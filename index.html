01 / ABOUT JEWELS

Our story. Our vision.

Discover the story behind Jewels Junior School, a family-centred Christian school committed to building tomorrow.

01 WHO WE ARE

Building a school with a vision.

Jewels Junior School was established in 2018. The idea originated from Nyasha and Tendai Vitorini as a family centred Christian School.

It was a Vision given to Tendayi who was a teacher by profession. The Vitorinis led by Nyasha then went to work in 2017 to build and establish the school.

The school is a family run institution through the Vitorini family trust.

2018

Established

Jewels Junior School was established in 2018.

126

Learners

Currently serving 126 learners.

Grade 7 Pass Rate

Our first Grade 7 candidates in 2025 achieved a 100% pass rate.

Our journey so far.

The school was registered with the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education as an ECD centre in 2018, then an infant school in 2019.

From 2020 it became a Junior school enrolling learners from ECD to Grade 7.

Our examination board is ZIMSEC and our first class of Grade 7 candidates was registered in 2025 scoring a 100% pass rate.

2017

The vision takes shape

03 / SCHOOL LIFE

Life at Jewels. Beyond the classroom.

At Jewels Junior School, learning is not limited to books and lessons. Our learners discover, play, create, interact and grow together.

01 / OUR CLASSROOMS

Where learning comes alive.

Our classrooms are designed to give learners a comfortable and supportive environment where they can ask questions, explore ideas and develop the confidence to learn.

THE CLASSROOM

A place to discover.

Every school day brings a new opportunity to learn something, develop a new skill and build friendships. Our classrooms are at the heart of that experience.

We encourage learners to participate, ask questions and become active participants in their own education.

02 / LIFE AT JEWELS

More than lessons.

01 / LEARNING TOGETHER

Growing together.

School is a shared experience. Learners work together, support one another and develop the confidence to express their ideas.

02 / SCHOOL COMMUNITY

A family-centred school.

Jewels Junior School is built around a family-centred approach where learners are encouraged to feel valued, supported and part of the school community.

03 / DISCOVERY

Curiosity matters.

We encourage children to look beyond memorising information and develop curiosity, creativity and independent thinking.

04 / CHARACTER

Building character.

Education is also about becoming a person of confidence, responsibility, integrity and good character.
